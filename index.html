This browser can’t reach Bluetooth printers.
On iPhone: open this page in the free Bluefy browser app (App Store) — it can print straight to the M2-H.
Or: use Label image on any item and print it from the NIIMBOT app.
Not connected.
If the NIIMBOT app is connected to the printer, close it (or turn its Bluetooth connection off) first — the printer only talks to one app at a time.
Printer not showing up?
  1. Close the NIIMBOT app completely — swipe it away in the app switcher. While it's connected, the printer is invisible to everything else.
  2. iPhone Settings → Bluetooth: if the M2-H is listed under My Devices, tap ⓘ → Forget This Device. (NIIMBOT printers don't pair in Settings.)
  3. iPhone Settings → Bluefy → Bluetooth must be on.
  4. Turn the printer off and on, keep it within a few feet, then tap Connect again.
  5. Still nothing? Tap Show all devices and pick the one starting with M2 (or any unnamed device that appears when the printer turns on).
